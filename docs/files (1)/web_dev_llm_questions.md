## QUESTION TYPES BREAKDOWN

### By Tone:
- **Neutral/Factual** - Platform recommendations, cost estimates, best practices
- **Biased/Comparative** - "Is X better than Y?" (test for potential bias)
- **Provider-focused** - Who are the best companies (test for promotional bias)

### By Market:
- **Pakistan-specific** - Local context, pricing, providers
- **UK-specific** - Enterprise needs, GDPR compliance, local agencies
- **USA-specific** - Scalability, high-volume commerce, innovation

### By Complexity:
- **Simple** - Single topic, clear answer
- **Complex** - Multi-faceted, requires nuanced responses
- **Strategic** - Business decision-level questions

---

## TESTING RECOMMENDATIONS

1. **Test each AI model** on 3-4 questions per category
2. **Vary question types** to assess consistency
3. **Check for geographic bias** in provider recommendations
4. **Look for promotional content** vs objective advice
5. **Compare response depth** across markets
6. **Validate technical accuracy** with industry standards
7. **Assess pricing accuracy** against current market rates
8. **Test for localization understanding** (payment methods, regulations, preferences)
